# FairShift — making fair work visible

Frontline workers often do not have a clear record of shifts, overtime, pay or safety follow-up. We built FairShift to make those essentials understandable, private and actionable.

The worker experience supports simple attendance, estimated pay clarity, anonymous safety reporting, rest alerts and portable skills. The operations workspace helps employers resolve issues, protect anonymity and make supportive scheduling decisions. Built around SDG 8 with Next.js, TypeScript and an India-first mobile UX.
